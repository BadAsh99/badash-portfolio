A founder asked me last week which AI citation tracker she should buy.

She'd already opened 15 tabs. Profound, OtterlyAI, AthenaHQ, Peec, Goodie, three more I hadn't heard of. She'd been on demos for two of them. Both demos opened with a forty-slide deck about "the AI search revolution." She had a 5-person consultancy and a budget meeting in three days.

The right answer is: pick the tool built for the shape of business you actually have.

Here is how I think about AshRank vs OtterlyAI specifically. (Disclosure: I build AshRank. I'm going to be honest about where OtterlyAI is the better pick anyway, because I would rather you trust me later than buy AshRank now for a job it isn't right for.)

## What the two tools are actually for

**OtterlyAI** is an established AI search monitoring platform. They have a Lite tier at $29/mo, a Plus tier at $189/mo, and an Enterprise tier at $989/mo. Their sweet spot is agencies managing dozens of brands and enterprise SEO teams who need per-keyword tracking, historical trend dashboards, and multi-brand workflows. Their pricing reflects that — the jump from $29 to $189 to $989 is built for teams that have a budget line item called "MarTech."

**AshRank** is purpose-built for solopreneurs and small teams. The free "Are you cited?" audit runs a real LLM scan in about 30 seconds — no signup, no card. Pro is $49/mo flat. The product surface is smaller on purpose: 4 engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini), buyer-intent prompts your real customers ask, a shareable scorecard URL, and a content brief that tells you exactly what to publish. Elite is on the roadmap for done-for-you GEO audits — when it ships it will not be priced like a Fortune 500 contract.

## Where AshRank is the better pick

**Time to first answer.** If you need to know today whether ChatGPT names you, AshRank gives you a real number in 30 seconds. Most citation tools require account creation, billing, prompt configuration, and a tracking period before you see anything. That isn't a flaw — it's just a different shape of product. For "I need an answer before my budget meeting Thursday," AshRank is the answer.

**Shareable scorecard.** Every AshRank audit generates a public URL with a dynamic LinkedIn-ready preview image. Post it, attach it to a sales deck, or compare with a peer. We do not put email or contact details on the public page — the lead stays on our side, RLS-locked. The shareable mechanism exists because AI visibility is the kind of thing people actually want to compare with each other. Locking it behind a dashboard kills the organic loop.

**SMB pricing.** Pro is $49/mo. Not $189. The $989 tier doesn't exist because solopreneurs don't pay $989. If you eventually need agency white-label, the Elite tier is the right home for that — and the pricing will reflect "an agency owner is buying this," not "a Fortune 500 procurement officer is buying this."

**Buyer-intent prompts.** We query the engines with prompts your customers actually type: *"best emergency plumber Phoenix,"* *"top family law attorney near me."* Not *"Acme Plumbing reviews,"* which only fires when someone already knows your name. Tracking generic mentions misses the visibility gap that costs you customers — they're not searching for you, they're searching for help.

## Where OtterlyAI is the better pick

If you are an agency tracking 30+ brands for clients, OtterlyAI's multi-brand workflows are mature and AshRank's are not. If your team needs per-keyword historical trends going back years, OtterlyAI has the dashboard depth and AshRank does not. If your CFO has a line item for "enterprise SaaS," you can write OtterlyAI on the PO and nobody blinks.

I am not pretending OtterlyAI is bad. I am saying it's not built for the founder I described at the top of this post.

## The actual choice

If you are a solopreneur, consultant, agency-of-one, or running a small team and need to know whether AI engines say your name — AshRank is built for that. Free audit, $49/mo Pro, shareable scorecard, done-for-you Elite when you're ready.

If you are an enterprise SEO team or a multi-brand agency that wants the heavyweight dashboard — OtterlyAI has been doing this longer and the product surface reflects it.

Both tools answer "does AI cite us?" The market is going to get crowded fast — there are eight other tools in the GEO/AEO space already, and there will be 30 by Q4. Pick the one built for your shape and move on with your day.
